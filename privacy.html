Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Zomine Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool operated by Vicky (@vickyzomine), the only user of the app.

YouTube (Google) data

The app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists on the owner's own YouTube channel. The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer. No YouTube or Google data is sold, shared or used for any other purpose. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Instagram (Meta) data

When someone comments a keyword on one of @vickyzomine's reels, Instagram sends the app that comment (comment ID, text, the commenter's username and ID). The app uses it only to send one private reply with the requested link and an optional public reply, and keeps a minimal local record (comment ID, time, outcome) to avoid sending duplicates. This data stays on the owner's computer, is never sold or shared, and is not used for advertising or profiling.

Data deletion

To have any record about you deleted, email vickyzomine@gmail.com with your Instagram username; it will be removed within 30 days. You can also remove the app's access to your own accounts at any time.
